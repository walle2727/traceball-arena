Rules

  • Move one point in any of 8 directions.
  • No line segment can be reused.
  • Margin lines are already traced; only margin points may be used.
  • Landing on a visited point, wall, or black gate-mouth dot gives another move.
  • The black dot in the middle of each gate line is already a bounce point, even before anyone visits it.
  • Score in the opponent gate. Own gate = own goal.
  • If a move timer is enabled, each move or bounce gets a fresh clock.
  • If time expires, no line is drawn; the ball stays put and the turn passes.
  • Two timeouts in a row pause the game; the board is hidden until play resumes.
  • Either joined player can pause or resume during a round.
  • If you are stuck, you lose.
